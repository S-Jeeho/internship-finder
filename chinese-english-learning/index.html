如果国宝会说话

"Every Treasure Tells a Story" (also translated as "If Treasures Could Talk")
This site is an independent English-learning project inspired by the documentary series 如果国宝会说话, produced by CCTV. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the broadcaster. The episode transcripts here are original sample text written for demonstration purposes, based on public-domain facts about each artifact — not verbatim excerpts from the show. To study with the real broadcast narration, please use an authorized/licensed copy of the documentary and add your own transcript files following the same JSON format (see the project README).

Pick an episode below. Each one shows the Chinese and English transcript side by side, breaks down useful sentence patterns and vocabulary, and ends with a short translation assignment you can try yourself.

Built for English learners studying through Chinese cultural documentaries.
